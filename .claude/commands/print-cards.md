STOP AND ASK THE FOUNDER before any order or payment. Also remind them: selling cards that use real people's
names commercially needs a lawyer's review of name/publicity rights in the target markets first.
1. Collect print files (825x1125 @300dpi with bleed) for $ARGUMENTS.
2. Build a print PDF: one card per page, fronts then backs in matching order, trim at 2.5x3.5in, 0.125in bleed,
   crop marks optional; convert colours to CMYK (note likely Sodium/Prussian shifts; make a proof page with swatches).
3. Write publish/print/README.md: specs, card count, and a checklist (legal review, printer spec match, proof).
