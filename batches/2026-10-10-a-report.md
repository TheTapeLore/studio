  worst drop is the median, so it isn't a lucky or unlucky pick.

  the registry caught it.

- Lore 007 and 010: every legend line is paraphrased from a source in the spec. Cut as unverifiable: Minervini's stop
  numbers (not seated) and a popular O'Neil "1961 post-analysis" story. O'Neil's fund slide uses his own explanation:
  too many small stocks, growing too fast.

**Teaching (the clarity gate).** Each spec's `learn` points are found on screen and logged in `episodes/<id>/qa.md`.

and "cup with handle" are defined in the captions.
