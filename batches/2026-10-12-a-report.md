**Computed, not drawn.** Every chart in this batch is real data, and every number is computed from it:
- **Lore 011:** Netflix vs the S&P 500, 2007–2010. The spec asserts the +176% and +36% from the data before it renders.
- **Lore 012:** 20 real daily ranges each for Coca-Cola and Tesla, December 2019.
- **Lore 013:** the 2007 distribution days are computed by the stated rule (−0.2% on higher volume, 25 sessions, removed after a 5% rally). Nothing is placed by hand.
- **Lore 014 and 015:** Google, September 2007. The Replay hides the name and never shows a bar past the frozen day.

**New in the engine:**
- **Components:** RangeBars, a component for daily ranges and ADR%. DistributionDays, a chart overlay with a live count.
- **CandleChart:** a % axis for split-adjusted history, and RS-high labels.
- **Score:** the CandleChart interpreter now follows each beat's window and gives distribution days their falls.
- **Replay format:** the Reveal gets its own tape label (THE REVEAL). The registry allows a Replay and its own Reveal to share a chart. validate_publish checks the X and Story polls and writes them into the copy files.
