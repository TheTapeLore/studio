# Content strategy: The Tape Lore

## Target audience
Self-directed traders 1–5 years in, trading stocks and starting on options or futures. They have read some
O'Neil or Minervini, watched breakout videos, and still have inconsistent results. Their real pains:
one big loss erasing many wins, not knowing how much to put on, selling winners too early and losers too late,
trading in bad markets, leverage that hurt them, and doubting their own discipline.
What they lack is not more setups but **clear mental models**, which is exactly what motion teaches best.

## Positioning
The calm, visual library in a loud feed. Not signals, not P&L, not hype. Every entry is a numbered Lore that
teaches one idea in under a minute, computed from real data or simulation. *Drawn from data, not hindsight.*

## Topical authority map
Search on YouTube, Instagram and X rewards accounts that cover a topic deeply. Each pillar is a "pillar topic",
each cluster grows to 5–15 entries, and every entry links to its prerequisites and next steps.
Full map: content/pillars.yaml. Seed ideas: content/backlog.yaml.

## Search intent mapping
| Intent | Example searches | Our format |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | "what is a VCP", "ADR percent", "theta" | lexicon (15s), then anatomy |
| How-to | "how to set a stop loss", "position sizing formula" | anatomy, whatif |
| Comparison | "options vs stocks", "win rate vs risk reward", "O'Neil vs Minervini" | whatif, council |
| Story / people | "Jesse Livermore", "Qullamaggie strategy" | legendcard, council |
| Pain | "why do I keep losing", "when to stay out of the market" | failure, operator anatomy |

Rule: the YouTube title and the first line of the Instagram caption use the searcher's words (backlog
`search_terms`); the on-screen hook uses our voice.

## Prioritisation
- **Quick wins**: high value, low competition, cheap to make (risk math, sizing, lexicon terms, failure files).
- **Big bets**: high search demand, crowded topics where our motion is clearly better (VCP, cup with handle,
  options payoffs, Council episodes, What If Lab).
- **Fill-ins**: lexicon entries produced in bulk to build authority and interlinks.
- **Avoid**: news, predictions, "stocks to watch", anything live.

## Internal linking plan
- Every entry: "Lore N. Next: Lore N+1" on screen; prerequisites and next in descriptions; X first reply links
  the prerequisite.
- Pinned X post and IG highlight "Start here" = the Map (/map), refreshed monthly.
- One YouTube playlist per pillar + "Start here". Every Short is added to its pillar playlist.
- Monthly Chapter and X Article stitch a cluster into one lesson and link back to each Lore.
- Replies: answer questions with "We drew this in Lore NNN". Each answer is a library visit.

## Success metrics (review monthly; tune the next season)
- Saves/bookmarks per view: the library thesis. Target rising month over month.
- Shares/sends per reach, completion rate (shorts > 70% for < 45s), rewatch rate.
- Profile visit → follow conversion; YouTube search impressions share; returning viewers.
- Production: 3 videos + 1 card per week shipped, 0 compliance failures, registry duplicate rate 0.
- Leading indicator for later monetisation: replies asking for deeper material, card requests, email signups (later).
