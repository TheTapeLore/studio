## Caption

A 60% win rate that loses money. A 35% win rate that makes it. Meet Mostly Right and Mostly Wrong.

Mostly Right wins 60% of trades. Small wins ($100), big losses ($200). Feels great most days.
Mostly Wrong wins 35%. Small losses ($100), big wins ($300). Feels terrible most days.

We ran both 1,000 times (simulated, 100 trades each, same coin flips):
Mostly Right: median −20%. Only 8% of runs end up.
Mostly Wrong: median +40%. 97% of runs end up.

Per trade, Mostly Right averages −$20 and Mostly Wrong averages +$40. How often you win is half the math. How much you win and lose is the other half.

The honest part: Mostly Wrong's usual worst streak is 9 losses in a row, and some runs hit 14. Trade like that and you'd better have planned for it.

Being right feels great. It doesn't pay rent.
