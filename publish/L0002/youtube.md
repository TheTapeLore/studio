## Title

Wins 60%, still loses money: win rate vs payoff | Lore 002

## Description

Mostly Right wins 60% of trades and still loses money. Mostly Wrong wins 35% and makes it. Below: the math, and the cost.
We simulated both traders 1,000 times (100 trades each, same coin flips): Mostly Right's median run ends down 20%, Mostly Wrong's ends up 40%. Per trade: 60% × $100 − 40% × $200 = −$20, and 35% × $300 − 65% × $100 = +$40. The cost of being Mostly Wrong: a usual worst streak of 9 losses in a row.
