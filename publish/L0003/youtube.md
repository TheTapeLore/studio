## Title

Made $500. Good trade? R-multiples explained | Lore 003

## Description

You made $500. On $100 risk that's +5R. On $2,000 risk it's +0.25R. Same dollars, very different trade.
R is what you lose if your stop gets hit: (entry − stop) × shares, set before you buy.

Measure every result with that one ruler. Entry $50, stop $48, 50 shares: 1R = $100, and $58 is +4R. Losers should all be −1R; a −3R means the stop moved or price gapped through it. Counting in R also makes accounts comparable: +3.5R is $350 on a $10,000 account risking 1% and $35,000 on $1,000,000.
