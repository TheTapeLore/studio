## Caption

Legend 01: Jesse Livermore. At 14 he posted stock prices for $5 a week. Swipe for the card and his three rules.

He traded stocks and commodities from the 1890s to 1940, made fortunes and lost them more than once. So study both halves.

What he wrote down, paraphrased:
1. Wait for the pivotal point, the level that decides the move. Then act.
2. Never average a loss. Add only to a winner.
3. Sit tight once you're right. That's the hard part.

Our Sodium tripwire, the line that snaps in every setup we draw, is his pivotal point.

Sources: How to Trade in Stocks (Livermore, 1940); Reminiscences of a Stock Operator (Lefèvre, 1923).
