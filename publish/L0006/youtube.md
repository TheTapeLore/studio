## Title

Risk 2% vs 0.5% per trade: −23% vs −6% drawdowns | Lore 006

## Description

Same 200 trades, sized two ways. Risking 0.5% a trade, the worst drop was −6%. Risking 2%, it was −23%.
The edge never changed. The size did, and losing streaks did the rest.

We simulated 1,000 traders with the same edge (40% winners, wins pay 2R, losses cost 1R). At 2% a trade the typical worst drop was −23%, and almost 1 in 5 runs fell past −30%. At 0.5% the typical worst drop was −6%. Half the runs hit 9 losses in a row: about −17% at 2% a trade, about −4% at 0.5%. Pick a size whose worst drop you could sit through, before the streak arrives.
