## Caption

Where does your stop go? Five legends, two camps, one thing they all agree on.

Your stop is the price where you sell and take a small loss. Here's how five legends set it, paraphrased from their own books and interviews:

• Livermore: watch how it acts after you buy. If it doesn't act right, get out.
• O'Neil: sell any stock 7 to 8% below what you paid. No exceptions.
• Darvas: just under the low of the range (his "box"), set the moment he bought.
• Kovner: where the market shouldn't go if you're right, and buy less so the dollar risk stays the same.
• Seykota: cut losses, cut losses, cut losses.

Camp one uses a fixed percent. Camp two uses the chart. Where all five agree: decide the exit before you buy, keep the loss small, and when it hits, go.

Our take: put the stop where your idea is wrong. Too far away? Buy fewer shares (Lore 005).
