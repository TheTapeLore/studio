## Title

Stop loss placement: 5 trading legends answer | Lore 007

## Description

Where does your stop go? Five trading legends, two camps, one rule they all share.
A fixed 7–8% under your buy, or just under the range the stock broke out of. Either way: decide it before you buy.

Livermore watched how a stock acted after he bought. O'Neil cut every loss at 7 to 8% below his buy. Darvas set his stop under his box the moment he bought. Kovner placed it where the market shouldn't go if he was right, and bought less to keep the dollar risk the same. Seykota: cut losses. All paraphrased.
