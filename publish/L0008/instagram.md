## Caption

Got an edge? One number answers that: expectancy.

Expectancy is what you make or lose per trade, on average. Measure it in R (1R = what you lose when the stop hits) and it works at any account size.

How to get it:
• Add up every trade's result in R.
• Divide by the number of trades.
• 20 simulated trades, +3.5R in total: +0.18R per trade.

Above zero, you have an edge. Below zero, you're paying to play.
