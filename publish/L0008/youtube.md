## Title

Expectancy explained: do you have an edge? | Lore 008

## Description

Got an edge? One number answers that: expectancy, what you make or lose per trade, on average.
Add up your trades in R and divide by how many there were. Above zero is an edge.

20 simulated trades, +3.5R in total: +0.18R per trade. The long way gives the same answer: win rate × average win − loss rate × average loss.
