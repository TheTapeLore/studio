## Title

William O'Neil: cup with handle and the 7-8% rule | Lore 010

## Description

William O'Neil turned $5,000 into $200,000, then measured what over 1,000 big winning stocks had in common.
What made him a legend, his cup with handle, his 7–8% sell rule, and the mistake he explained himself.

Why a legend: he studied stocks by computer in the 1960s (his firm built the first computerized daily stock database), measured over 1,000 big winners back to the 1880s, and shared it with everyday investors in Investor's Daily (1984). His method: buy leaders as the price breaks above a sound base, like the cup with handle, on heavy volume; cut every loss at 7 to 8%. His miss: his fund rose 116% in 1967, then shrank from $49 million to $6 million by 1975; in his words, too many small stocks, growing too fast.
