## Caption

The market fell 56%. Netflix rose 66%. Same 17 months, October 2007 to March 2009.

The line that flagged it is relative strength: a stock's price divided by the market (here the S&P 500). It rises when the stock beats the market and falls when it lags.

What happened:
• Late 2008: stocks crashed, and Netflix fell 51% from its April high too.
• Then it bounced back much faster than the market.
• December 2008: its RS line hit a new 52-week high while the price was still 35% below its own high.
• 14 weeks later the price broke to a new high.
• Over the next 15 months: Netflix +176%, the S&P 500 +36%.

The market was on fire. Netflix was mailing DVDs.

A new RS high while the price lags is a clue for your watchlist. It isn't a buy signal on its own.
