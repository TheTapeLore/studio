## Title

RS line: Netflix +66% while the market fell 56% | Lore 011

## Description

October 2007 to March 2009: the S&P 500 fell 56%, Netflix rose 66%.
The RS line (price divided by the S&P 500) hit a new 52-week high in December 2008, 14 weeks before the price did.

Relative strength compares a stock with the market. In late 2008 Netflix fell hard too, then rebounded much faster than the market; its RS line made a new high while the price was still 35% below its high. After the price broke out it rose 176% in 15 months, against 36% for the S&P 500. Treat it as a watchlist clue, not a buy signal.
