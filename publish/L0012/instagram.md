## Caption

Why do some stocks need a wider stop? Because some stocks move three times as much on a normal day.

ADR% (average daily range) is how far a stock moves from its low to its high on a normal day, averaged over 20 days.

Last 20 days of 2019:
• Coca-Cola: 0.9% a day
• Tesla: 2.9% a day
• A 2% stop: reached on 0 of 20 Coca-Cola days, and 15 of 20 Tesla days

Same stop, very different odds. Tight stops on wild stocks get hit by everyday noise. If the stop has to be wider, buy fewer shares (Lore 005).
