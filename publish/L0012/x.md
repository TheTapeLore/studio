Why do some stocks need a wider stop?

ADR%: how far a stock moves, low to high, on a normal day.

December 2019: Coca-Cola 0.9%, Tesla 2.9%. A 2% stop was never reached on a Coca-Cola day. Tesla reached it on 15 of 20.

## First reply

ADR% = the 20-day average of (high ÷ low − 1). A stop tighter than one normal day gets hit by noise; a stop that fits needs a smaller position (Lore 005).
