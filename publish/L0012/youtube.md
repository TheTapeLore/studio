## Title

ADR% explained: why some stocks need a wider stop | Lore 012

## Description

Why do some stocks need a wider stop? ADR%: how far a stock moves, low to high, on a normal day.
December 2019: Coca-Cola 0.9%, Tesla 2.9%. A 2% stop: 0 of 20 Coca-Cola days reached it, 15 of 20 Tesla days did.

ADR% is the 20-day average of (high ÷ low − 1). A stop tighter than one normal day gets hit by noise; if the stop must be wider, size the position down.
