## Title

Distribution days: the 2007 top in 8 warnings | Lore 013

## Description

A record high in October 2007, then the warnings piled up: 1, then 5, then 8 distribution days.
Five months later the S&P 500 was down 19%; by March 2009, 57%.

A distribution day is a day the index closes down 0.2% or more on higher volume than the day before. Count them over the last 25 sessions (a day drops off early if the index rallies 5% above it). O'Neil's rule of thumb: four or five in about a month is trouble. It's a warning, not a forecast.
