## Caption

The mystery stock was Google, in September 2007. If you voted buy, the first six weeks were a joy.

What happened next:
• Bought at the September 21 close, it never closed below the buy price
• Six weeks later it was up 32%
• Then the market topped and heavy-volume down days piled up (Lore 013)
• It gave the gains back, week after week

Two endings for the same trade:
• Exit on a close below the 50-day average (the average close of the last 50 days): sold in December, up 20%
• Hold and hope: down 25% by March 2008

The buy was the easy half. Plan your sell before you need it. The 50-day line is one example, not the only rule.
